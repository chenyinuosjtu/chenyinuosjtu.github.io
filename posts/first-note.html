Note 001

Why a public notebook?

July 2026

Writing privately is easy to postpone. A thought can stay vague in a notebook, a draft, or the back of my mind, and no one asks it to become clearer. Making something public changes the stakes a little. It asks me to write more often, say what I mean more accurately, and take responsibility for the ideas I put into the world.

I also hope this notebook can be a small signal. The internet is enormous, but a precise thought has a way of finding the people who have been thinking about something similar. I do not expect to have finished answers here. I hope instead to make room for conversation, recognition, and the occasional useful disagreement.

Most of all, I am making this for myself. Writing is one of the few ways I know to turn a loose collection of impressions into something I can examine. It helps me notice what I think, where I am uncertain, and what is worth returning to. This notebook is a place to leave that trail: articles, questions, fragments, and ideas still in progress.

That is enough reason to begin.
